# Yummy Dips II research notes

**Checked:** October 14, 2026. Primary sources were prioritized. Toast-hosted order and location information is dynamic; recheck before public launch.

## Location distinction and correction

The first concept used the primary Yummy Dips website’s listed **711 Lydig Ave, Bronx** address. That was the wrong location for the requested Steinway Street site. The current concept is explicitly for **Yummy Dips II at 25-59 Steinway St, Astoria, Queens**. These are separate location listings and must not be conflated. Steinway Street is in Astoria, Queens, not the Bronx.

## Menu copy and caveats

The official Yummy Dips main [menu page](https://yummydips.net/menu) returns broad menu highlights via search-indexed results, but the location-specific Yummy Dips II page did not expose its menu content during research. The concept uses a small set of **brand-menu highlights** and ingredient descriptions, including:

- **Yummy Dips Special Crepe:** milk chocolate, strawberries, blueberries, bananas, white-chocolate drizzle, and vanilla ice cream.
- **Fruity Pebbles Waffle:** white chocolate, Fruity Pebbles, and strawberries.
- **Dubai Yummy Ice Cream Dip:** dipped mini waffle, milk chocolate, strawberry ice cream bar, pistachio sauce, crushed pistachios, kunafa, fresh strawberries, and two scoops of ice cream.
- **Dubai Cheesecake Dip:** Brooklyn-style cheesecake, milk chocolate, pistachio sauce, crushed pistachios, and kunafa.
- **Cup-in-cup drinks:** branded signature drink presentation; no specific flavor is claimed.

The page calls these menu highlights and directs visitors to Yummy Dips II’s location-specific Toast page to confirm what is currently offered in Astoria. No prices are shown. Availability can vary by location and time.

## Other operational notes

- **Rewards:** Yummy Dips’ main loyalty page says 1 point per dollar and $5 off every 50 points. A branch-specific Yummy Dips II rewards program was not verified, so this concept does not promote or promise rewards.
- **Payment note:** Yummy Dips II’s order page showed a 3% card surcharge at research time. It is not advertised here; confirm current checkout terms before launch.
- **Hours and ordering:** Never hard-code weekly hours or imply the order page is always accepting orders. Link visitors to the live Yummy Dips II order page.
- **Menu page:** Yummy Dips’ main menu page can be protected by reCAPTCHA; no local Astoria menu inventory was verified.

## Supplied visual references and design decisions

The six supplied images informed the berry/cherry-pink/misty-rose/blue palette, oversized type, playful sticker energy, and large food photography. The palette reference lists Burgundy `#6C0820`, Cherry Blossom Pink `#F2AEBC`, Misty Rose `#F2DCDB`, Silver Lake Blue `#5A86CB`, and Lapis Lazuli `#3D5D91`; these remain the core colors. Reference brands, wording, and exact layouts are not copied.

The current hero uses a hard-edge rectangular photo rather than an arched/window silhouette. The site adds more visible hover and pointer response, responsive menu tabs, keyboard navigation, a touch-first photo strip, reduced-motion behavior, and a Queens-specific location feature. This is an independent concept, not the official site and not deployed.
