## Facts used on the page

- **Address:** 242 Knickerbocker Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11237.
- **Phone:** (718) 766-0635.
- **Published hours:** 11:00 AM–10:00 PM daily, 7 days a week, according to Ayat’s Bushwick page. The concept also includes a note to confirm holiday/special-service hours. A third-party listing surfaced different late-week closing hours; the official Ayat listing was preferred, but the restaurant should confirm before launch.
- **Ordering:** Ayat’s Bushwick page links to the location-specific Order Online page above.
- **Food/identity:** Ayat describes itself as Palestinian soul food and highlights za’atar, traditional dishes, and shared family-style meals. The location page says the restaurant is all halal and welcomes walk-ins.
- **Story:** Ayat’s About page identifies founder Abdul Elenani and discusses the restaurant’s Palestinian roots and family story. Page copy paraphrases these points; it does not use an invented quotation.
- **Menu:** The official menu preview names Mezze Filistini, the wood-fired oven section, M’sakhan, Mansaf, Maklouba, Lamb Ouzi Royale, and family platters. It explicitly cautions that items and prices may change and vary by location. The pitch page avoids prices and repeats that availability should be checked with the live menu or Bushwick ordering page.
- **Reservations:** The official reservation information says walk-ins are welcome; Bushwick reservation inquiries should be directed to the location by phone. The page links to the reservation page without promising a particular booking slot or capacity.
- **Catering:** Ayat’s Bushwick page promotes family-style catering and links to its catering ordering page.

## Creative direction

The supplied Mochi Matcha Bar, Pure Bite, and Enka food/social references informed the oversized editorial type, food-first hierarchy, and warm/green palette. Their branding, content, and layouts were not copied. The visual direction is anchored in Ayat’s Palestinian identity, Bushwick location, real venue photography, and verified menu language.
