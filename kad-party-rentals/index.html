05 GOOD TO KNOW

The details
make the day.

These answers summarize KAD’s public policies and FAQ. Terms can change—confirm the current details when requesting your quote.

Read KAD’s published policies ↗
Is delivery available?+

KAD’s public FAQ says optional delivery is available for rental orders of $100 or more, with the charge based on location. Standard service is curbside and a reasonably short distance; stairs, elevators, longer carries, exact-time, after-hours, or same-day round trips may add fees. Confirm your address and delivery window directly with KAD.

Does setup come with the rental?+

KAD’s FAQ says tents and bounce houses generally include setup and takedown in the rental fee. Concession equipment is operated by KAD staff. Tables and chairs may be set up for an additional fee; arrange that in advance and confirm labor availability.

What is the rental period?+

KAD describes rates as covering a reasonable event period and says Saturday or Sunday equipment is usually due the following Monday. Confirm the return date and rental period for your specific item and event.

What does the public policy say about payment?+

KAD’s public policy page states that a 50% deposit is required to confirm a reservation and is nonrefundable; the balance is due on the event day before delivery or pickup. These terms may be old—verify payment terms directly with KAD before paying.

What about damage or weather?+

KAD’s published policy says equipment should be returned in the same condition and that the renter is responsible for security and weather-related damage. Ask KAD to explain the current policy and any protections or responsibilities for your event.

Does sending this list reserve the items?+

No. The list is an inquiry only. KAD must confirm current inventory, rates, delivery options, and booking terms before anything is reserved.
