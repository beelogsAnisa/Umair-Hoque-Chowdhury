# KAD Party Rentals — research notes

**Research snapshot:** 2026-10-16 (America/New_York)  
**Purpose:** Ground a sales concept in the business’s own public material while clearly marking stale, conflicting, or third-party information.  
**Operational-status confirmation:** The user confirmed on 2026-10-16 that KAD is currently taking bookings. The public sources reviewed did not independently prove recent booking activity; this is recorded as user confirmation, not an independently verified fact.

## Primary-source findings

### Business identity and contact

- KAD’s official Wix homepage says it has served customers since 2006, describes a broad party-rental selection, and identifies the location only as **Brooklyn, NY**. It lists **(347) 538-6809** and `kadpartyrental@gmail.com` in its main contact area. A separate homepage section lists `kadpartyrentals429@gmail.com`; the two addresses conflict. The concept uses the main-contact email and phone, and does not display a street address.
- The official site has a `/quote` page asking visitors to complete a form and saying KAD will respond. It is important not to describe the existing site as having no quote form. The concept offers a more guided inventory shortlist, but its email-draft flow is not connected to the existing Wix form.
- The homepage links to Instagram `@angpartyrentals`; the official photo-gallery page links to `@kareem_durham`. These handles differ and current ownership/activity could not be verified. Neither is linked from the concept.
- The homepage says “Since 2006”; this is a claim from KAD’s own public site, not an independently audited credential.

### Public delivery and policy language

KAD’s public delivery FAQ says optional delivery is available for orders with at least $100 in rental value, with the charge depending on location. It describes standard delivery as curbside and a reasonably short distance; stairs, elevators, longer carries, exact-time, after-hours, or same-day round-trip service may incur additional fees. The page says customers may request delivery/pickup dates.

The public FAQ says tent and bounce-house rentals generally include setup/takedown; concession equipment is operated by staff; table/chair setup can be arranged for an additional fee in advance, subject to labor availability. The policies FAQ says rental rates cover a reasonable event period; most Saturday/Sunday rentals are due the following Monday; a 50% nonrefundable deposit confirms a reservation; the balance is due on event day before delivery/pickup; and the renter is responsible for returning equipment in the same condition and for security/weather damage.

These are historical public terms, not legal review or confirmation of current policy. The website paraphrases them as **KAD’s published FAQ/policy** and instructs customers to confirm directly.

## Current activity and discrepancy checks

- The official quote page remains reachable and invites quote inquiries, but the broader sitemap/pages show a 2020 last-modified date. A functioning page by itself does not prove that requests are currently being accepted.
- Search results surfaced an **unclaimed Yelp listing** for “ANG Party Rentals” with the same phone number and an “Updated August 2026” label. A separate MapQuest listing carries similar information. Because these are third-party records and the business naming/address relationship is not confirmed on KAD’s official site, they are treated only as leads. The concept does **not** use the street address shown by those listings.
- Attempts to fetch both Instagram profiles and the Yelp detail page were blocked (HTTP 403); targeted handle searches did not surface current activity. No recent customer booking or owner-post was independently verified.
- The user subsequently confirmed KAD is taking bookings. The preview is therefore being prepared as a pitch concept, while the site still makes clear that each visitor’s request requires direct confirmation.

## Design and product decisions

- The supplied “Woodland Retreat” reference informed the dark editorial photography, spacious type, and split-image sections. The “Japan Tours” reference informed warm image cards and a clear multi-step path. No unrelated branding, text, or source screenshot assets are copied.
- The concept focuses KAD’s category breadth in one browsable catalog and gives customers a quantity-based inquiry list. It does not invent online availability, instant booking, a service-area radius, delivery quote, or guest-capacity recommendations. The visual KAD monogram is a text treatment for this concept, not an official logo.
- Photos were downloaded from KAD’s own public Wix image CDN and optimized into local WebP assets in `/assets/`. The final project includes only optimized files; large original downloads are not bundled. Before launch, KAD should approve image reuse and supply current, high-resolution images if desired.
- External references reviewed for category/quote-flow context: [Brooklyn Party Rental homepage](https://www.brooklynpartyrental.com/) and [All Borough Party Rentals homepage](https://allbororentals.com/). Their content informed broad UX context only; no copy or branding is reused.
