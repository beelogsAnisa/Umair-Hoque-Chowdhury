# KAD Party Rentals — concept pitch notes

## One-line idea

A warmer, clearer way for a customer to go from “I need a few things for my event” to a well-prepared rental inquiry—without pretending the website can confirm inventory or book the order instantly.

## Why this direction fits KAD

KAD’s public pages describe a broad mix: tables, chairs, tents, an inflatable, concessions, linen, food-service equipment, costumes, and a PA system. This concept places that breadth in a single searchable catalog, rather than asking a visitor to piece together the inventory from separate category pages.

The visual direction uses the actual photos available from KAD’s public site: dark editorial hero photography and split sections, balanced with warm occasion cards, a clear three-step path, and an image-led gallery. The supplied screenshots shaped the design rhythm, not the brand or copy.

## What a visitor can do

1. Browse or search the public-site catalog and filter by category.
2. Add quantities to a persistent quote list.
3. See an indicative item subtotal for priced entries and flag items with no public rate.
4. Add an event date, occasion, location, contact details, and delivery/setup notes.
5. Prepare an email to KAD or copy the request; the page states this is an inquiry, not a reservation.

## What is deliberately not promised

- Live inventory or date availability.
- A confirmed booking, delivery route, final total, or instant order.
- A specific service radius, street address, or current social account.
- Current prices where the public pages could be stale.
- Staffing, setup, or delivery beyond what KAD’s published FAQ says.

## Demo-to-launch checklist

- Confirm all rates, units, item names, and in-stock products with KAD.
- Confirm primary contact email, phone, operating status, and social account; resolve the two email addresses and two Instagram links.
- Confirm the delivery threshold, access surcharges, setup terms, rental duration, deposit, and payment terms.
- Obtain approval for the existing photos or replace them with current approved photography.
- Replace the demo `mailto:`/clipboard path with a secure form endpoint if desired; test submissions and data handling with KAD.
- Verify mobile layouts and accessibility against the real catalog, policies, and contact workflow before launch.
