# Rent A Party NY — concept pitch notes

## Why this prospect

The supplied official Wix site presents rentals across chairs, tables, tents, inflatables, concession machines, and extra equipment/services. Its contact page provides a quote/contact path and says the company serves all five NYC boroughs. The tent page states that the business has been family-owned since 2014. Those are the site’s claims, not independently verified credentials.

The official site is live and its public table-rental page directs customers to contact the business in advance to reserve tables. The page sitemap reports a December 2025 modification date, and the store-category sitemap reports August 2026. That supports proceeding with an **inquiry-focused concept**, but does not prove a particular event date is available or a booking is confirmed.

## Concept direction

- Keep the polished, responsive structure and useful interactions from the KAD concept while building in a separate folder; do not overwrite KAD.
- Give Rent A Party NY a brighter coral-and-citrus accent system and event-oriented copy.
- Organize the published rental/service categories into searchable filters and a quantity-based request list.
- Ask for the event date, borough/venue, item quantities, contact details, and access/setup notes in one place.
- Use “Request a quote” rather than checkout, a booking calendar, or instant reservation.
- Omit item prices: the public service pages do not give rates by rental item. The separate Wix store page contains 12 generic “I’m a product” entries and placeholder category text, with sample prices that cannot safely be matched to actual rentals.
- Use only the phone/email and five-borough service statement confirmed on the supplied official site. Avoid unsourced hours, exact address, delivery promises, fees, deposits, or booking terms.
- Include actual published table sizes and a water-slide dimension only with clear reminders to confirm the current model and capacity.
- Do not use placeholder team names or testimonial copy from the current Wix pages.
- Include the official site’s “family-owned since 2014” claim only as a site-attributed fact; the prototype labels it as such.
- Keep Instagram absent until the business verifies its official account. Similar-name accounts/domains are not enough to establish ownership.

## What the prototype improves

- Replaces generic store-product names and an unfinished category description with a clear list built from the service pages.
- Cleans up visible copy/spelling without inventing new service claims.
- Provides category browsing, item search, quantity selection, and a clear quote-request flow.
- Is responsive across mobile/desktop and includes keyboard-accessible controls, focus states, a skip link, and reduced-motion handling.

## Demo boundaries and launch checklist

The quote list is a front-end interaction only. It saves items in the visitor’s browser and prepares a draft email; it does not contact the company automatically. The concept is not an official website or booking system.

Before any launch, ask the business to approve:

1. Their official brand/logo, contact details, service area, and social links.
2. Current item names, variants, images, sizes/capacities, availability, and itemized prices.
3. Delivery/pickup/setup, rental-period, damage/weather, payment, deposit, cancellation, and booking terms.
4. Their preferred request destination and whether the quote form should connect to a real inbox/CRM or booking system.
5. Rights to use images from the public Wix site, or a set of approved replacement photos.

## Instagram status

**Unverified / not found.** The official Wix homepage source did not contain an Instagram URL, the site did not visibly link a profile, and exact business/contact searches did not establish an account. Do not guess a handle or link a similarly named company. Ask the business which account, if any, is official.
