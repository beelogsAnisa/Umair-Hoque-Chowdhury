05 GOOD TO KNOW

A few details
before the party.

These answers summarize Rent A Party NY’s public pages. Confirm current item options, rates, service, and terms with the team when requesting an estimate.

Browse the public services page ↗
What can I rent?+

Rent A Party NY lists folding and round tables, cocktail tables, plastic and cushioned chairs, chair covers and sashes, tents, bouncers, water slides, cotton-candy and popcorn machines, crushed-ice machines, chocolate fountains, generators, and steamer trays. It also lists tent and bouncer repair services.

Which areas do you serve?+

The public site says Rent A Party NY serves New York City’s five boroughs: Manhattan, Queens, Brooklyn, the Bronx, and Staten Island. Share your venue address so the team can confirm details for your event.

What table sizes are listed?+

The public table page lists foldable plastic tables in 4-, 6-, and 8-foot lengths; 48- and 60-inch round tables; and a 30-inch cocktail table. It also lists approximate guest counts for some sizes. Confirm the current item and capacity with the team.

Can I see prices online?+

The service pages direct visitors to contact Rent A Party NY for an estimate. Ask the team to confirm current pricing, availability, delivery and setup details for your date and quantities.

Does sending this list reserve the items?+

No. This concept prepares an inquiry only. Rent A Party NY must confirm current availability, pricing, delivery/setup details, and booking terms before anything is reserved.
