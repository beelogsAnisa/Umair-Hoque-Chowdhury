## Current operating / booking signal

- The official Wix contact/quote form is live at fetch time; the public table page instructs visitors to contact the company in advance to reserve tables. The service pages and sitemaps are also live and date-stamped.
- **Interpretation:** the public primary source supports proceeding with an inquiry-focused design concept. It does not prove that the business is open every day, taking a particular booking, or has any specific item available. The demo therefore says “request a quote,” not “book now,” and explicitly says that a request is not a reservation.
