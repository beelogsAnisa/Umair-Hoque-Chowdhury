Most restaurant photography below was downloaded from image URLs embedded in Puglia's public site at [pugliaofnyc.com](https://www.pugliaofnyc.com/) (Squarespace CDN), then resized/re-encoded for this review concept. This is a provenance note, **not** a license grant. Public availability does not prove permission for commercial reuse. Obtain approval or replace those images before launch.

The hero pizza is AI-generated illustrative concept art, not a Puglia photograph or confirmation of a specific dish. `assets/pizza-hero-cutout.png` has a true alpha channel; the slate/checkerboard backdrop is removed. Replace it with current restaurant-approved photography or real footage before production. The earlier generated source remains at `research/generated-pizza-hero-source.png`.
